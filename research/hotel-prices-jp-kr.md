# Mid-Range Hotel Prices — Japan & Korea (researched late 2025 / early 2026)

Scope: typical **mid-range double room** (3-star / business-class to lower-4-star), per night, baseline (non-peak) pricing.
Conversions (Dec 2025 avg): **1 CNY ≈ 22 JPY; 1 CNY ≈ 207 KRW; 1 USD ≈ 7.1 CNY**.
  - sources: https://www.exchange-rates.org/exchange-rate-history/cny-jpy-2025 · https://www.exchangerates.org.uk/historical/CNY/31-12-2025
"CNY range" below = approximate low–high band for a standard double. Sources cited per table.

## 1. TOKYO

## Unsourced / flagged items
