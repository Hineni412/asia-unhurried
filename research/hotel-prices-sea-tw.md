# Mid-Range Hotel Price Research — SEA + Taipei (data: late 2025–2026)

**FX anchors (Feb 2026):** US$1 ≈ ¥6.9 | NT$1 ≈ ¥0.22 | ฿1 ≈ ¥0.21 | RM1 ≈ ¥1.68 | S$1 ≈ ¥5.3 | ₫1,000,000 ≈ ¥276
(source: exchange-rates.org USD/CNY history, https://www.exchange-rates.org/exchange-rate-history/usd-cny-2026)

"Mid-range" = typical 3-star / simple 4-star / boutique double room, per night, before local taxes (Singapore/Penang add ~10–19% at checkout).

**Citywide mid-range double: ~¥400–800/night** (US$60–115; avg ≈ $65, high-season ≈ $122)
Source: https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/hotels/taiwan/taipei-1668341 ; KAYAK 3-star avg $87–136: https://www.kayak.com/Taipei-City-Hotels_3-stars.Hts.19888.hotel.ksp

| Zhongshan–Yongkang (Zhongshan/Da'an) | 2,400–7,000 | ¥530–1,540 | **+15–30%** — hotel-dense, Michelin/design stock |

Sources: https://www.faroway.ai/blog/where-to-stay-in-taipei-first-time-visitors (NT$ bands by area); https://hotelping.net/en/magazine/taipei-ximending-zhongshan-xinyi-hotel-guide-2026 ("Ximending cheapest hotel area"); https://www.dghotel.com.tw/en (D.G. **Seasonality**
- **Peak:** Lunar New Year week (late Jan–Feb) — sharpest spike; Feb is most expensive month on KAYAK (~¥1,460/night avg vs ~¥280 in Jun). Also Oct–Nov autumn + year-end.
- **Low:** Jun–Sep heat/typhoon season and post-CNY lull — **−20–35%** vs peak.
Sources: https://www.kayak.co.uk/Taipei-Hotels.r4383.dr.html ; https://tripbudgetcalculator.com/blog/the-cheapest-month-to-visit-taipei-and-why/ ; https://www.trustedtrips.ai/en-gb/region/taipei.hq-l99

Sources: https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/hotels/vietnam/hanoi-1581130 ; https://gotripzi.com/destinations/hanoi-vn/cost

- **Low:** May–Aug monsoon **−30–40%** (Jul–Aug cheapest).
Sources: https://gotripzi.com/destinations/hanoi-vn/cost ; https://travelguidestip.com/best-time-to-visit-hanoi/ ; https://daytripsvietnam.com/guides/vietnam-tet-effect-2027/

**Mid-range double: ~¥800–1,500/night** (US$115–220 / S$150–280; BYT mid avg $116, high-season avg $208). Add 9% GST + 10% service.
Sources: https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/hotels/singapore-SG ; https://singaporehotelsradar.com/guides/hotel-in-singapore-best-areas-costs/

| Neighborhood | Mid-range double | ≈ CNY | vs. baseline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chinatown | S$180–240 boutique | ¥950–1,270 | **Baseline** — best value-vs-character mix |
| Little India–Bugis | S$120–200 (Little India cheapest central district) | ¥640–1,060 | **−20–35%** |
| Marina Bay | S$250–450 "accessible" / mostly S$380–800+ | ¥1,325–2,385 | **+50–100%+** — little true mid-range stock |

Sources: https://travelguidestip.com/where-to-stay-in-singapore/ (Chinatown S$180–240; Marina Bay avg S$485); https://www.singapore-spirit.com/guides/where-to-stay-singapore/ ; https://breezesingapore.com/where-to-stay-little-india/

**Seasonality**
- **Spike:** F1 night race (Sep; 2026 race ~early Oct per calendar shift — verify year) — citywide ARR hit record S$325; race-circuit hotels **2–3×** (e.g., Pan Pacific S$1,625 vs S$400 a week later). CNY + Nov–Dec festive **+20–40%**.
- **Low:** post-CNY Mar–Jun; Jun cheapest (~−20%, ~S$270–280 avg).
Sources: https://www.businesstimes.com.sg/singapore/f1-race-sends-singapore-hotels-average-room-rate-takings-record-highs-september ; https://mothership.sg/2024/09/hotel-price-up-to-3-times-higher/ ; https://malaymail.com/news/singapore/2024/09/13/singapore-hotels-around-f1-street-circuit-more-than-doubled-in-price-for-race-weekend-suites-go-up-to-over-rm18000-per-night/150260 ; https://singaporehotelsradar.com/guides/hotel-in-singapore-best-areas-costs/
